Settings

Controls

iPhone: left side drag = move / steer & throttle. Right side drag = camera. CAR = enter or exit (steal) a car. RUN = sprint (in a car: handbrake). JUMP = jump (in a car: brake/reverse). 🔫 = attack. ⇄ = switch fists/pistol. 📯 = horn.

Controller: left stick move, right stick camera, A jump/brake, B sprint/handbrake, Y car, RT shoot/accelerate, LT brake, X switch weapon, Start pause.

Keyboard: WASD move, mouse drag camera, Shift run, Space jump/handbrake, F car, J or click shoot, Q weapon, H horn, Esc pause.

Map: yellow = missions, green $ = Spray Shop (repair + lose cops, $100), blue H = Hospital, purple S = Safehouse (save), red = Police. Cash, health and armor pickups spawn around town.
